## Why `competitor-gaps-research-hook` was caught here and missed under Spark

Not the candidate gate — the gate never sees a `research_hook` (V2-011). The
research budget is **3 searches per 10 minutes** and `research-business-idea`
is under three minutes long, so the whole conversation gets three slots, and
what decides the moment is which three ideas take them.

- Here the observer's first hook asked **one** question — "how large is the
  meeting software market *and* who has raised in this niche?" — covering both
  halves of the planted moment, leaving two slots. The second (competitors
  beyond transcription, 98.2 s) became `packet-2`, which the judge matched to
  `competitor-gaps`.
- Under Spark the observer split the same request into **two** hooks (market
  size, then funding) at 67.3 s, and a third ("what do they offer today?") took
  the last slot at 94.1 s. The ideal question — "what do users complain about
  for Otter, Fireflies and built-in notetakers?" — arrived at 113.8 s, was
  approved by the research gate at **0.90**, and was never searched: no budget.

So the sharpest lever left on research recall is `observer.md`: **one hook per
thing the group asked for.** Splitting a single out-loud request into two
questions costs a search slot and, on a three-minute conversation, a moment.
Both research misses in both runs — `venue-logistics-research-hook` too — are
budget and timing, not wording: `offsite-plan`'s hook window closes at 83.6 s,
and nothing was approved there in either run.
