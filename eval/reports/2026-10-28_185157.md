`server/eval/ceiling.ts`) replays each capture through the turn assembler
alone — no model, no network, no session folder — and prints, per planted
moment, the window on the session clock, when the observer's look lands under
the PRD's board cadence, and the longest silence the transcription reported
between the two. Reruns cost nothing, so any future change to the cadence or
to `PAUSE_MS` can be checked before an eval run is paid for.

### The only cadence that clears 0.7 costs more than the e2e budget

`npm run eval:ceiling -- --every N` prices the one fix attempt 1 identified —
letting the observer look more often than the board updates:

| look every | 250 ms | 300 ms | 400 ms | 500 ms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 final turn | 0.83 | **0.83** | **0.83** | 0.67 |
| 2 final turns | 0.67 | 0.50 | 0.50 | 0.33 |
| 3 final turns | 0.50 | 0.33 | 0.33 | 0.17 |
| 4 final turns (shipped) | 0.33 | 0.17 | 0.17 | 0.00 |

Only a look on **every** final turn clears 0.7, and only at a threshold that is
still recognisably a pause. Every two turns tops out at 0.67 — and that 0.67
needs `PAUSE_MS` 250, i.e. ~0.71 s of real quiet, which is under the 400 ms
this run used.

That is where it stops being a tuning question. `npm run e2e` (US-028, PRD
US-021) measured 16 observer looks across its four fixtures; at a look per
final turn the same four conversations (24, 26, 11 and 11 final turns) need
**72**, so 56 extra calls. This run's own figures price a call at
$1.0773 / 98 = **$0.011**, so that is about **+$0.62** on an e2e run that
currently costs $0.4826 against a $0.50 gate `runPassed` enforces — roughly
2.2× the budget, not a margin problem.

So PRD US-016's recall gate and PRD US-021's cost gate cannot both hold with
the observer tied to the board cadence. Resolving that is a product decision,
and the three ways out are all outside this story:

1. **Decouple the observer from the board and raise the e2e budget.** 0.83
   recall at `PAUSE_MS` 300–400, and e2e at roughly $1.10.
2. **Make a per-turn look cheap.** The cost is reasoning tokens, not input, so
   this means a smaller or non-reasoning call for the look itself — a second
   model tier the PRD does not have.
3. **Widen the planted windows.** `deadlineLine` is `triggerLine + 4` in five
   of the six moments, which is exactly the board loop's own period, so the
   fixtures ask for a verdict within one board cycle of the trigger. Six lines
   would make the measurement about judgement instead of cadence. Forbidden
   here by criterion 2.
