## The failed gate is the research budget, not the gate under test

The candidate gate never sees a `research_hook` (V2-011), so `--gate spark`
cannot have cost the research moment directly. What happened is in
`research.jsonl`: `research-business-idea` gets **three search slots** (3 per
10 min, and the conversation is under three minutes), and the observer spent
two of them splitting one planted moment into two hooks — "what is the annual
market size for meeting software?" at 67.3 s and "have any startups in this
niche raised serious funding?" at 67.3 s, where the Glimmer run asked both in
one question. A third slot went to "what do Otter, Fireflies and built-in
notetakers offer today?" (94.1 s). The question that would have caught
`competitor-gaps-research-hook` — "what do users complain about for Otter,
Fireflies and built-in notetakers?" — arrived at 113.8 s, well inside the
110.1–138.1 s window, scored **0.90** at the research gate, and was never
searched because there was no slot left.

`venue-logistics-research-hook` is missed in both runs, so the three-moment
research gate is really being decided by one conversation, in steps of 0.33.
Read it as noisy rather than as a verdict on either gate.

## Why this run is committed even though it fails

V2-020 asks for both reports, and the comparison is the point: the local gate
costs two moments of recall, removes one unmatched glow, and adds ~24 s of
judgment that this harness cannot charge it for. The default stays Glimmer
because this report, the gateless one, is the one that fails — see
`tasks/decisions.md`.
