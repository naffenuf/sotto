A **one-fixture verification run** for V3-010, not a full eval. It exists to show
that the new scoring measures what it says it does, end to end, without paying
for the whole nine-fixture set:

- The positive control planted in `rhetorical-questions` (the Loom per-seat
  price, the one real lookup among twenty-eight rhetorical questions) is caught
  by Sotto **saying** the answer, and the judge matched the sentence to the
  moment. Speak recall 1/1.
- The claim is scored apart from the catch, as V3-015 asks: "I'll check Loom
  per-seat pricing" is in the `claimed` column, not in the recall.
- Nothing was said about any of the other questions in the script, so the
  false-speech gate passed with two unprompted lines on the stage.
- Both lines show at **80.5 s** and both latencies read as ~0: that is the
  `max` replay's clock, which does not move inside a tracked model call. The
  same artifact is why the "nothing spoken over a human turn" gate reports
  `not measurable` here and the `over` column reads `?`. `npm run e2e` is the
  only clock that can answer it.
- The read-back the group's final decision earned was composed and then not
  said (`frequency`), which is V3-013's rule for a bare read-back at
  `normal`: the decision reached the board either way.

Cost: 55 model calls, 3 judge calls, $0.59 for the one conversation.
