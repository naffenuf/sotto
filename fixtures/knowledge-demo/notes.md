# Northvane Labs — internal notes

_Fictional. Northvane Labs is an invented company; nothing in this file describes a real
business, customer or person._

## Pricing

- The **Pro** plan is **$40 per seat per month** on the list price sheet. It was $49 for
  the first two quarters of 2026 and came down in July; anyone quoting $49 is quoting the
  old sheet.
- Annual prepay takes 20% off Pro and 25% off Enterprise. Sales may add at most 15% on
  Pro and 25% on Enterprise without Priya signing it off.
- Starter has no discount at all. We have said no to that four times this year.

## Renewals

- Renewal dates live in `customers.csv` and nowhere else. If a date is being argued
  about in a meeting, that file settles it.
- Brightsend Logistics is the only account with a procurement review, and it runs for
  about nine weeks before the renewal date.
- Harborview Clinic will not renew without SSO. That is the one commitment we have made
  in writing.

## Support

- Two escalations on one account in a quarter is the churn-risk threshold. Nordgren
