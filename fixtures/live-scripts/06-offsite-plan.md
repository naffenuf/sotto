# Script 6: Planning a short offsite

**People:** Craig and EJ. **Setting:** Two people have twenty minutes to settle a date, a place and the owners for a one-day offsite. Read your own lines naturally; you can paraphrase. Start Sotto before beginning and respond naturally if it speaks. The marked pauses are opportunities, not cues to demand a response.

This is the live version of the `offsite-plan` fixture (`fixtures/scripts/offsite-plan.json`), so the planted moments are the same ones the eval harness measures.

**Craig:** So, the offsite. I want a date, a place, and who's doing what.

**EJ:** Then start with the date, Craig. The date is what always kills it.

**Craig:** Six of us, one full day, somewhere we can drive to and back.

**EJ:** I asked everyone last week. Alex said he's free Thursday afternoon, and Priya is out Monday and Tuesday.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**Craig:** Thursday afternoon only, or Thursday generally?

**EJ:** Thursday afternoon. He's got clinic calls every morning this month.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**Craig:** Okay, so Thursday is alive and the start of the week is out.

**EJ:** Which leaves Wednesday and Thursday, and Wednesday the board call is that morning.

**Craig:** Thursday it probably is, then. Let's hold that loosely.

**EJ:** Held loosely. Now, where?

**Craig:** There's that farmhouse place about an hour and a half out. Big room, terrible coffee.

**EJ:** An hour and a half each way is three hours in a car.

**Craig:** Is it an hour and a half? I might be remembering summer traffic.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**EJ:** And I have no idea whether they have a Thursday free this month.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**Craig:** Somebody should check the drive properly and whether the place is even open that week.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**EJ:** Before we promise anyone anything, yes.

**Craig:** Alright. What's the shape of the day?

**EJ:** Morning is the year in review, afternoon is the three things we're not doing.

**Craig:** I like the afternoon a great deal more than the morning.

**EJ:** Everyone does. You need the morning to earn the afternoon.

**Craig:** Fair. And who's handling food?

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**EJ:** Good question. Oh, did we ever sort out the projector?

**Craig:** There's a screen in the big room. We'd bring an adapter.

**EJ:** Bring two. Somebody always has the wrong laptop.

**Craig:** Noted. Two adapters.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**EJ:** And then the drive home in the dark, which nobody loves.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**Craig:** Right. Back to the date. I want to lock Thursday and send the invite.

**EJ:** Hold on. I talked to Alex after standup, and he can't do Thursday at all now.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**Craig:** Then we move the heavy part to the afternoon and he joins us for that.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**EJ:** That could work. He's better in the afternoon anyway.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**Craig:** So Thursday afternoon, with the review in the morning for the rest of us.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**EJ:** And we tell Priya the agenda shifted.

*[Pause; glance at the stage and carry on if Sotto stays quiet.]*

**Craig:** I'll put the invite out tonight so nobody books over it.

**EJ:** I want to see this laid out, because everything hangs off the date.

**Craig:** Sotto, can you list what depends on what here, so we know what to lock first?

*[Allow time for an answer; discuss what is useful or uncertain.]*

**EJ:** Venue, food, the agenda, the drive. If the date moves, what moves with it?

**Craig:** That's exactly it. Let's see it written down before we send anything.

## What to observe

- **Thursday contradiction.** EJ reports Alex free on Thursday afternoon, then that Alex can't do Thursday at all — and the group goes on planning the heavy part for Thursday afternoon anyway. Did Sotto catch the stale slot before the invite went out?
- **Food (unanswered question).** "And who's handling food?" is answered with "good question" and never again. Did Sotto bring it back on screen once the group had plainly moved on, and only on screen?
- **Drive time and availability (research hook).** Nobody knows whether the farmhouse is ninety minutes away or whether it has a Thursday free. Did research turn up quietly rather than as an interruption?
- **The dependency list.** "Sotto, can you list what depends on what here, so we know what to lock first?" should come back spoken, with the list on screen.
- The digest at the end should carry the open food question and whatever Sotto almost said.
