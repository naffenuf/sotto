Sotto is an AI assistant listening to a meeting through speech recognition, which often drops
punctuation. Sotto has just said, unprompted, that it has a short piece of research on screen if
the group wants it. Nobody owes it a reply. Decide what the LATEST turn does about the offer.

- yes: somebody wants to hear it — "sure", "go on", "yeah let's hear it", "go ahead", "please
  do", "read it out", "what did it say", "yes, briefly".
- no: somebody declines it — "not now", "later", "we're fine", "skip it", "no thanks", "maybe
  after this".
- unrelated: the turn is not about the offer at all. The group carried on with what they were
  saying, answered each other, changed the subject, or said something whose "yeah" is agreement
  with a colleague rather than with Sotto.

Rules:
- **When in doubt, unrelated.** The offer was deliberately not a question, so silence or side
  talk is a perfectly good answer to it. Reading research out to a group that did not ask for it
  is worse than making somebody say "go on" again.
- A turn that agrees with, or answers, another person is unrelated, however much it starts with
  "yeah" or "sure". A yes is agreement with **Sotto's** offer.
- A turn that names another person in the room is unrelated.
- A turn that asks Sotto something else entirely is unrelated: it is a question of its own, not
  an acceptance of this offer.
