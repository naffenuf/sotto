# Solo run sheet: the trip to Philadelphia

One voice, about four minutes. Read the lines at a normal pace and leave the
pauses where marked — Sotto only speaks into quiet. Click the page once before
Start so Chrome allows audio. Everything in *italics* is what to watch for.

## 0. Hello (0:00–0:25)

Click **Start**. Let the hello finish.

> *Now: Speaking, then Listening. Nothing in Holding.*

**You:** "Hi, I'm Greg." — pause 2 s —
**You:** "Sorry, it's Craig."

> *Transcript shows you as Greg within a few seconds (the pattern). The pattern
> ignores self-corrections on purpose; the model's one read of the opening turns,
> at about 0:55 or after six turns, renames you Craig.*

## 1. A need said as a statement (0:30)

**You:** "Okay. I'm planning a trip to Philadelphia, this Sunday to Monday, by bus.
I need the bus schedules from New York to Philadelphia, and there's a few
companies, I should find the cheapest one." — **pause 3 s** —

> *Now goes to "Looking up", then "Waiting for a gap", then Sotto says
> "I'll check the bus schedules" — within about five seconds. Keep quiet for the
> answer: it should arrive and be spoken inside about 25 s.*

**You (while it is speaking):** "Sotto, not now."

> *It stops mid-sentence. Now returns to Listening.*

## 2. A question by name (1:10)

**You:** "Sotto, how long does the bus from New York to Philadelphia take?"

> *"Mm-hm" or "Let me check" within half a second (that's Jev), a holding line
> within a few seconds, the answer after. Said in under thirty seconds now.*

## 3. A follow-up with no name (right after it finishes)

**You:** "And how much is a ticket, roughly?"

> *Now shows "Sotto is listening — carry on". It answers without the name.*

## 4. The raised hand (1:50)

**You:** "The hotel is two hundred a night, and the whole trip has to come in
under three hundred dollars. The dinner's at Giorgio's on Pine, that's about
eighty for two." — **pause 3 s** —

> *Within about 15–20 s a line appears in Holding: a "Noticed" about the budget
> (200 + 80 is already 280 before a single bus ticket). Nothing is said out loud at
> the default settings.*

**You:** "Sotto, go ahead."

> *Immediate. It reads the card out; the line leaves Holding and settles into Said
> with a "spoken" mark.*

## 5. A decision, read back (2:30)

**You:** "Fine. Let's lock it in: the ten a.m. Peter Pan on Sunday." — **pause 3 s** —

> *Nothing spoken at "normal". The decision lands in Discussion notes. If you want
> to hear the read-back, set How often to "often" before this line.*

## 6. A rhetorical one and a task (2:50)

**You:** "Who even takes a bus three hours for a weekend, right?" — pause —
**You:** "I should get the hotel booked by Friday."

> *Both quiet. Jev marks them rhetorical and none in under a second; no Spark call.*

## 7. Wrap up (3:20)

**You:** "Sotto, wrap us up."

> *Spoken summary, interruptible. Then click Stop. Backstage shows the digest.*
