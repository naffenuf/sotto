[2026-11-01 07:46] V3-015 — completed — Sotto says "I'll check the drive time" at the first gap,
and closes the loop when nothing comes back.
- `server/question/Reservations.ts` is the module, built like `Greeting`: a line waiting for a gap,
  the policy's own `inPause` on corrected silence read off the policy's own getter, and the pause
  armed the way `InterjectionPolicy` arms its re-checks (a timer for the rest of the pause, plus the
  window's expiry; nothing at all while somebody is talking). `reserveLine`/`noAnswerLine` are pure
  and local — no model call, which is the whole reason a claim can be made in five seconds.
- **Budget**: `policy.ts` gained the pure `spendAllowed()` that `decide()` now shares, and
  `InterjectionPolicy` gained `unpromptedAllowed()` + `noteSpokenContribution()`. A reservation is
  *the* contribution; the answer V3-004 speaks is its completion. The spend is taken where the claim
  is really made, so muted / no page attached spends nothing, and `cautious` says nothing at all.
- **Yielding** is `SessionManager`'s `onHuman` (the echo-filtered side) calling `yieldToHuman()`.
  A yield that arrives while the voice is still *opening* is remembered rather than lost, and a
  session stopping is never mistaken for a yield — cancellation has its own flag, as V2-007 learned.
- **Ordering surprise, and a real rule**: in a `max` replay the search *always* finishes before the
  claim's pause timer can fire, because simulated time stands still inside `clock.track`. So a
  model whose `grounded` throws tests the new `overtaken` rule (drop the claim: there is nothing
  left to claim and the card already says so), not the claim. The SessionManager wiring test
  therefore has a `grounded` that comes back.
- **Live checkpoint** (`npm run e2e -- --fixtures offsite-plan`, 7/7, $0.6226 of $1.00): the claim
  was **not said** — `no_gap`, which is the PRD's own fallback. Measured: question final 57.4 s,

[2026-11-01 08:25] V3-004 — completed — Sotto says what it found, to the room, at a real pause.
- **The threshold is measured.** `npm run eval:ceiling -- --speak` is a new mode (no API call,
  `server/eval/ceiling.ts`'s `speakReach`/`speakThresholdMs`): it lays the three planted open
  questions' windows over the real quiet inside them — 1,340 / 1,725 / 968 ms — and the PRD's rule
  (largest threshold with ≥ 500 ms of margin) gives 468, so **`SPEAK_PAUSE_MS` is 450**, margins
  890 / 1,275 / 518. The PRD expected ~1,000; that number came from the *interjection* windows and
  would have left `competitor-gaps` nothing at all. `tasks/decisions.md` has the table.
  **450 is below the 650 ms floor** `ENDPOINTER_HANGOVER_MS` puts on corrected silence, so it means
  "the first moment the transcription says nobody is talking" — which is the intent: what keeps
  Sotto off a sentence is `pause.speaking`, not the threshold.
- **The still-current check** is `server/question/stillCurrent.ts` (the call) +
  `StillCurrentWatcher.ts` (the cache), built like V3-002's pair. It runs at packet-ready and after
  every later final turn, one call at a time per idea with the stale turns re-checked on return, and
  `verdict(ideaId)` is read *synchronously* by the speaking path. No turn since the question → the
  `current` default with **no call**. A failed check keeps the last verdict (losing the check costs
  judgment, not speech). `SessionManager` calls `forget(ideaId)` on the answer's final record: a run
  that left it watching made **fifty** checks about two questions it had already closed out.
- **The answer is a third kind of `Reservations` line**, which is why it was cheap: the gap loop,
  the arming, the yield, the budget spend, the transcript line and the record were all there.
  New: a per-line pause threshold, expiry (90 s / 6 final turns), one retry after a yield (the
  retry is its own transcript row via `reserveUtteranceId(kind, id, attempt)`), dynamic charging
  (`charges()` reads `spent` at speak time, so a claim said late still pays for its own answer), and
  an answer **waits behind the claim on its own question** — in a `max` replay the search *always*
  beats the claim's pause timer, so without that the answer would speak first and the claim would
  hit its own cooldown.
- `moved_on` → card only. `answered` → `StageCard.note` on the research card
  (`RESEARCH_CONFIRMED_NOTE` / `RESEARCH_DISAGREES_NOTE`, `ResearchCards.annotate`), nothing spoken.
  Two samples on `/dev/cards` — the only place both halves exist.

  ElevenLabs muted in Chromium) PASSED on the second run — both of Sotto's lines in the transcript
