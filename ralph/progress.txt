[2026-11-01 07:30] V3-003 — completed — a question the room wondered aloud becomes a search at
once, with a card on the stage saying so.
- `ResearchManager.acceptQuestion` is the whole path: dedupe, then **straight into the run
  queue**, unshifted ahead of the planner's and the hooks' ideas, with **no gate call at all**
  (`--gate` is irrelevant here; the classifier made that judgment). The budget is shared
  unchanged. It returns `accepted | reused | duplicate`, and `SessionManager.noticeQuestion`
  — still the one call site — is what acts on it.
- `ResearchIdea.askedAtMs` is the one piece of history an idea carries, because the thing being
  measured spans the path: `ResearchPacketRecord.questionToPacketMs` is stamped on **every**
  outcome, so V3-015's "nothing came back" has a latency too.
- `ResearchPacket.spokenAnswer` is in the same reply as the rest (no second call): required in
  the strict JSON schema, **optional in zod**, so a model that omits it loses the line rather
  than the packet. `research-packet.md` asks for it as something heard once.
- `ResearchCards` grew three methods and no new rules: `pending()` (thinking card now, id
  `research-<ideaId>`, outside `CARD_ID_PREFIX` like every research card), `accept()` filling
  that same card in with no pause and no budget — `filledIn: true` — and `failed()`, because a
  spinner that never stops is worse than a short no. `reuse()` brings an earlier packet's card
  forward. `ResearchCardRecord.cardId` is now written down rather than derived, since a
  question's card id is not `researchCardId(packetId)`; `server/eval/runner.ts` reads it.
